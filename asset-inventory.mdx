Your **Monitored Assets** page lists every system associated with your organisation — both the ones you declared and any new ones your audit team discovered along the way.

## Your Asset Inventory

From the **Assets** page, you can browse, search, and filter all your assets.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/asset-inventory-overview.png" alt="Rifteo Monitored Assets page" noZoom />
</Frame>

You can filter by:

- **Asset Type** — e.g. API, Web
- **Discovered only / Non Discovered only** — to isolate assets your audit team found versus the ones you declared
- **Environment** — e.g. Production
- **Created At** — by date

## Declared vs Discovered

Assets in your inventory fall into two categories:

- **Declared** — assets you provided yourself when setting up the audit scope (see [How Audits & Scope Work](/audit-creation-scope)).
- **Discovered** — new assets your audit team found during testing that weren't part of your original scope, such as a forgotten subdomain or an exposed staging environment.

Use the **Discovered only** filter to quickly see everything your audit team found that you hadn't declared yourself.

## Creating an Asset

Click **Create an Asset** to add a new asset to your inventory.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/create-asset-form.png" alt="Rifteo Create an Asset form" noZoom />
</Frame>

**Basic details:**
- **Type** — e.g. Web, API
- **Name**
- **Environment** — Development, Production, Pre-production, or Staging
- **Endpoint**
- **Port**
- **Description**
- **Core Functionality** — list the asset's primary functionalities, to help scope its complexity for future audits

**Advanced Settings** (optional):
- **Asset Relationships** — link this asset to related ones
- **Test Accounts** — provide test accounts with appropriate roles; these can be edited anytime later

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why do I see assets I never declared?">
    These are likely **Discovered** assets — systems your audit team found during testing. Use the **Discovered only** filter to see them all at once.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I add assets myself?">
    Yes, using the **Create an Asset** button on the Assets page.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Who can view the asset inventory?">
    Both Organisation Admins and Organisation Members can view it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
