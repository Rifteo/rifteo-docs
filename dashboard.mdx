## Client Portal Overview

Your dashboard greets you with a summary of recent activity and key metrics, along with quick access to your audits, attack surface, and security events over time.

## Key Cards

At the top of your dashboard, key cards summarize your current security posture:

| Card | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Registered Assets** | Total number of assets in your inventory |
| **Audit Groups** | Number of audit groups |
| **Total Audits** | Total number of audits run |
| **Discovered Findings** | Total findings discovered across all audits |
| **Security Grade** | An overall letter grade summarizing your security posture |

Below the cards, you can switch between three views:

- **Overview** — a Security Events Timeline showing findings, remediations, and audits over time
- **Audit Calendar** — scheduled and ongoing audits
- **Attack Surface** — your monitored attack surface

<Tip>
On the Overview tab, use the **All / Daily / Evolution** toggle and the date range filter to adjust how the timeline is displayed.
</Tip>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why don't I see any data on my dashboard?">
    If you haven't run any audits yet, your cards and charts will show zero values until your first audit is underway.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What does my Security Grade mean?">
    It's an overall letter grade summarizing your security posture, based on the findings discovered across your audits.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is the dashboard different for Organisation Admins vs Members?">
    Both roles see the same dashboard overview. For a full breakdown of what each role can do, see [Your Role](/your-role).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
