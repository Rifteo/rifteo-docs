## Meet Teo

Teo is Rifteo's AI assistant. Alongside your audit findings, Teo analyzes your results and surfaces plain-English recommendations to help you understand what matters most and what to do about it.

Think of Teo as a knowledgeable second pair of eyes — it doesn't replace your audit team's expertise, but it helps you make sense of your results faster, especially if you don't have a security background.

## Where Teo Appears

You'll run into Teo in two places on your dashboard:

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/teo-copilot-sidebar.png" alt="Teo copilot panel in the Rifteo sidebar" />
</Frame>

- **Sidebar copilot** — a chat-style panel where you can ask Teo questions about your audits, findings, or overall security posture.
- **Recommendation badges** — small badges shown directly on findings, giving you an at-a-glance signal from Teo without needing to open the copilot.

## Understanding Recommendations

Each Teo recommendation is made up of two parts:

- **Risk assessment** — Teo's plain-English read on how serious a finding is and why, going beyond the raw severity label to explain the practical impact for your organisation.
- **Suggested action** — a concrete next step, such as which finding to prioritize first or what kind of fix is typically involved.

<Note>
Teo's recommendations are meant to help you prioritize and understand your results faster — they don't replace the detailed guidance provided with each finding, which remains the source of truth for how to fix an issue.
</Note>

## Acting on Recommendations

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a recommendation">
    Click on a recommendation badge on a finding, or ask Teo directly through the sidebar copilot.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the risk assessment">
    Understand why Teo flagged this as important for your organisation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow the suggested action">
    Use Teo's suggestion to help decide what to prioritize in your remediation work — especially useful when you have many findings and aren't sure where to start.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take action on the finding">
    Head to the finding itself to acknowledge, resolve, or request clarification, following the usual remediation flow.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does Teo replace my audit team?">
    No. Teo helps you understand and prioritize your results faster, but the audit team remains responsible for the findings themselves and the detailed guidance on how to fix them.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I ask Teo anything, or only about specific findings?">
    You can ask Teo general questions about your audits and security posture through the sidebar copilot, not just about individual findings.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Who can see Teo's recommendations — Admins or Members too?">
    Both Organisation Admins and Organisation Members can see Teo's recommendations and use the sidebar copilot.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if I disagree with a recommendation?">
    Teo's suggestions are meant to guide prioritization, not override your judgment. If something looks off, reach out to [support](/contact-support) and the team can take a look.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
