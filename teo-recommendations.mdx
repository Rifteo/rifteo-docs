## Meet Teo
 
Teo is Rifteo's AI assistant. Alongside your audit findings, Teo helps you make sense of your results faster — you can ask it questions about your audits, findings, or overall security posture in plain English.
 
Think of Teo as a knowledgeable second pair of eyes — it doesn't replace your audit team's expertise, but it helps you understand what's going on, especially if you don't have a security background.
 
## Where Teo Appears
 
You'll find Teo in the chat panel, available from any page in your dashboard — look for the yellow icon in the bottom-right corner.

## Understanding Recommendations
 
Open any finding and go to its **Recommendation** tab to see Teo's suggested fixes. If none exist yet, click **Generate Recommendations** to have Teo analyze the finding and propose some.
 
<Frame>
  <img src="/images/teo-recommendation-tab.png" alt="A finding's Recommendation tab showing the Remediation Effort chart and a list of Teo-generated recommendation cards" noZoom />
</Frame>
Each recommendation is a card with three parts:
 
- **Effort** — a quick tag (Low, Medium, or High) showing how much work the fix typically takes
- **Title** — a short name for the action, such as "Implement Centralized Logging"
- **Description** — one or two sentences explaining what to do
Above the list, the **Remediation Effort** chart plots all recommendations for the finding on a radar, so you can compare their relative effort at a glance.

## Acting on Recommendations
 
<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a finding's Recommendation tab">
    From your findings list, open a finding and click the **Recommendation** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate recommendations if needed">
    If none are listed yet, click **Generate Recommendations** to have Teo analyze the finding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compare effort and read each suggestion">
    Use the Remediation Effort chart and the Effort tags to see which fixes are quickest, then read each description to understand what's involved.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask Teo for more detail if needed">
    Open the chat panel (yellow icon, bottom-right) and ask Teo directly if you want more context on a recommendation or your overall posture.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take action on the finding">
    Head back to the finding to acknowledge, resolve, or request clarification, following the usual remediation flow.
  </Step>
</Steps>
