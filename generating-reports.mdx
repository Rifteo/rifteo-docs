Once your audit is complete, Rifteo generates a detailed report summarizing everything your audit team found. This page explains what's in that report and how to find it.

## Report Types

The type of report you receive depends on the audit category:

| Report Type | Based on |
|---|---|
| **Penetration Test Report** | A hands-on audit of a specific target |
| **Vulnerability Assessment Report** | An automated scan across your systems |
| **Attack Surface Assessment Report** | Ongoing monitoring of your exposed assets |

## Finding Your Reports

All your reports are listed on the **Reports** page.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/audit-reports-list.png" alt="Rifteo Audit Reports list" noZoom />
</Frame>

For each report, you'll see:

- **Audit ID** and **Audit Name** — to identify which audit the report belongs to
- **Primary Asset** — the main target the report relates to
- **Generated At** — when the report was created
- **Expires At** — the date after which the report will no longer be available for download
- **Status** — whether the report is currently `Available`

<Tip>
To download a report, click the small **report icon** next to it. The arrow (`>`) next to it instead takes you to the full audit page.
</Tip>

<Note>
Reports have an expiration date. Make sure to download the reports you need before they expire.
</Note>

## Generation Flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Audit marked Complete">
    Once your audit team finishes their work and all findings are published, the audit status changes to **Completed**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Report is generated">
    Rifteo automatically compiles your audit data — findings, severity, recommendations — into a structured report.
  </Step>
  <Step title="PDF delivered">
    The finished report is made available for download directly from your dashboard, with an expiration date.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What's Inside a Report

Every report follows a consistent structure, so you always know where to find what you need:

- **Cover Page** — audit name, dates, and scope summary
- **Executive Summary** — a plain-English overview of your overall security posture, written for non-technical stakeholders
- **Findings by Severity** — every finding, grouped from Critical down to Info
- **Recommendations** — practical next steps for each finding, to guide your remediation priorities

## Charts

To help you quickly grasp the overall picture, reports include visual summaries:

- **Severity distribution ring** — a breakdown of how many findings fall into each severity level
- **Criticality breakdown** — a visual view of where your biggest risks are concentrated

## ASM Reports — How They Differ

Attack Surface Assessment reports follow the same overall structure, with a few key differences:

| | Pentest / Vulnerability Report | ASM Report |
|---|---|---|
| **Scope** | Based on targets you declared | Based on continuously discovered assets |
| **Findings** | Tied to specific tested targets | Tied to your evolving external attack surface |
| **Frequency** | One report per audit | Can reflect ongoing/recurring scan results |

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How long is my report available before it expires?">
    Check the **Expires At** column on the Reports page — this date varies per report, so make sure to download reports you need before they expire.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="When will my report be available?">
    Your report is generated automatically once your audit is marked **Completed**, and can be downloaded right away from the Reports page.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What does my Security Grade mean?">
    It's an overall letter grade summarizing your security posture for that specific audit, based on the findings discovered.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does an ASM report look the same as a Pentest report?">
    The overall structure is similar, but ASM reports focus on continuously discovered assets rather than a fixed set of declared targets — see the comparison table above.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Who can access reports — Admins or Members too?">
    Both Organisation Admins and Organisation Members can view and download reports.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
